Collection Records

Bronze to Ascendant — what the hive remembers, and what it pays.

What is recorded

Strains assimilated, bosses felled, biomes survived, gene cards archived, equipment catalogued, organisms assembled — every category climbs a rank ladder as it completes.

The rank ladder

Unranked → Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Diamond → Ascendant. Each rank pays a permanent account bonus, making completion a real progression path — the details live in the in-game Collection screen, which always reflects your own record.
