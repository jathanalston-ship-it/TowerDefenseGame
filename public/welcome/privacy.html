Privacy Policy

PLACEHOLDER — have this reviewed and completed before launch. The statements below describe how the software actually behaves and are the honest starting point for counsel.

· Game progress is stored on your device (browser storage). Optional cloud backup stores an anonymous copy keyed by a random identifier you control — no name, email, or profile exists.

· Payments are processed entirely by Stripe; we never see or store card details. Purchase receipts are retained (offer, amount, timestamp, anonymous player id) for support and refunds.

· Analytics are aggregate counters (how many sessions/runs/claims per day) with no user identifiers, no cookies, and no cross-site tracking. Page analytics uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookie-free.

· Leaderboards store the display name you choose and your scores.

· Contact: PLACEHOLDER contact address.
