RELEASE · November 9, 2026

Let the hive take over

After Campaign Stage 5, Hive Intelligence can handle repetitive setup, waves, gene picks and hero abilities while you watch or take over.

Easy waves should not mean placing the same organisms over and over. Hive Intelligence is now an optional continuous assistant.

Unlock it after Campaign Stage 5

Complete Stage 5 on any difficulty, then turn on HIVE INTELLIGENCE in Campaign or Infinite. It works on new campaign stages as well as replays.

What the hive handles

  • Deploying towers, buying mutations and hero upgrades, and merging conveyor organisms.
  • Starting waves after the setup countdown and picking the recommended gene after a visible countdown.
  • Directing the lead hero toward threats and using their ability when ready.

You stay in control

Switch it off whenever you want, including during a gene draft. Pick a gene yourself to override the recommendation. Pause suspends automation, and the toggle starts off each run.

This is a simple helper, not a guaranteed win. Daily, Boss Rush, Quick Skirmish, tutorials and uncleared Threat levels remain manual. The hive does not spend permanent or premium currencies, sell your towers, or launch another run.
