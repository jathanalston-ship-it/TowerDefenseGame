THE FUTURE

Roadmap

What is live, what is next, and what is on the horizon.

This roadmap reflects current development priorities. Order and scope evolve with player feedback — the dev blog announces changes.

  • NOW — LIVE

    The living game

    Campaign, Infinite, Boss Rush and Daily runs; 15 tower strains; 5 heroes with equipment and forks; Mega Boss raids; the Living Battlefield; the scored opening cinematic; expeditions; collections and prestige.

  • NEXT

    Deeper roster

    New tower strains and heroes toward the long-term targets (100 towers / 30 heroes), more authored bosses and Mega organisms, and new biome ecosystems with bespoke terrain flora.

  • SOON

    Community & competition

    Expanded daily leaderboards, replay theater sharing improvements, seasonal events, and community spotlight features on this site.

  • PLANNED

    Steam release

    A Steam edition with achievements and cloud saves. Wishlist link lands on the homepage the day the store page is live.

  • PLANNED

    Mobile

    iOS and Android builds — the game is already touch-first and portrait-native; the remaining work is store packaging and platform polish.

  • EXPLORING

    The far horizon

    Co-op raid experiments, user-authored daily modifiers, and localization beyond EN/ES/PT.
